FILE A-3MOI · CairoFleet

Fleet readiness and fuel control for 200+ vehicles

Situation
A fleet of 200+ vehicles across 5+ sites ran 100+ movements a day. Unplanned downtime and high fuel use put time-sensitive operations at risk.
Action
Introduced predictive maintenance tracking of recurring faults and service history, with closure monitoring on every job. Set up fuel monitoring per vehicle and followed up on variances with drivers and workshops.
Result
About 35% lower fuel consumption, better fleet availability and less downtime across dispatch operations.

Action
Set FIFO/FEFO rules through governance and supervision, with briefings and audit follow-up.
Result
Stronger inventory accuracy and audit readiness.
